Use the proposals and objections to write a concise neutral settlement summary.  State the correct civil burden, the claim elements, and any necessary damages, credibility, limiting-purpose, digital-evidence, sympathy, or adverse-inference rule.  The summary must not argue the evidence, assume disputed facts, tell the jury which witness to believe, shift the burden, include excluded material, or use a party's proposed wording when an objection identifies a valid defect.

For disputed facts, use conditional wording such as `if you find`.  For compensatory contract damages, keep the instruction to damages caused by breach and proven with reasonable certainty.
