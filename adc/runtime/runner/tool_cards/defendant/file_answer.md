Answer the complaint directly.  Admit what the record requires, deny what is disputed, and state defenses that fit the pleaded claim and visible facts.  Use a concise `summary`; do not draft a paragraph-by-paragraph pleading unless the opportunity asks for one.  Do not verify signatures, audit damages, use byte reads, or conduct trial-proof work for an answer.  Preserve those disputes in the answer and develop them later.  Do not use the answer as a motion or a speech.
